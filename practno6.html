Tailwind Utilities

Four Important Classes

These classes control the alignment, size, weight and spacing of elements.

↔️

text-center

Aligns the text to the center of the element.

🔠

text-xl

Increases the text size to the Tailwind XL size.

B

font-bold

Makes the text bold by increasing its font weight.

↔

p-4

Adds 1rem (16px) of padding on all sides.

Practical Example

College Notice Card

Class Explanation

Class Purpose College Website Use
text-center Centers the text. Centering the college notice.
text-xl Makes text larger. Notice and section headings.
font-bold Makes text bold. College name and important dates.
p-4 Adds 16px padding. Adds space inside notice cards.
